Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: one 4 by 4 sprite atlas for the Norwegian school life-simulation game Livslina.
Primary request: create SIXTEEN separate isometric pixel-art diorama scenes for young adults studying, working and living independently. Image 1 is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match its detailed hand-pixelled 8-bit aesthetic, tiny expressive people, warm timber and cream walls, teal edge trim, crisp stepped pixel contours, muted colourful objects, soft neutral daylight, charming cutaway two-wall rooms on diamond-shaped floor tiles. New subjects, same visual family. No smooth 3D rendering, no blur.
Composition: square canvas, exact uniform 4-column by 4-row grid. One complete isolated diorama centred in each cell, with equal scale, equal generous transparent gutters, no overlap or cropping between cells. Each scene must be readable at 230 pixels wide. TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND outside the dioramas, no painted cream grid behind them. No labels, captions, lettering, numerals, logos or watermarks anywhere.
Scenes in strict reading order:
Row 1: (1) new neighbourhood community meeting: three young adults greeting around a small table with plants in a welcoming community room; (2) shared flat kitchen: two housemates making an inexpensive meal with vegetables and shopping bags; (3) household finances: young adult at desk sorting bills beside a phone and coin jar; (4) travel: young adult with backpack at a modest bus stop beside a bicycle.
Row 2: (1) student study group: three students around books and laptops in a library corner; (2) academic guidance: student and friendly tutor talking across a desk with notebook; (3) nursing practice: student nurse in blue scrubs learning with a supervisor by an empty examination bed; (4) teacher training: young adult leading a small classroom, mentor at side, board with abstract shapes only.
Row 3: (1) carpentry: two young adult craftspeople with timber, hand tools and a workbench; (2) IT support: new employee and mentor by two monitors and a small server cabinet; (3) retail work: worker at checkout, second worker stocking shelves; (4) warehouse: two workers distributing cardboard parcels at shelves and a hand trolley, no forklift.
Row 4: (1) cafe work: young adult barista with coworker at coffee counter, a friend at a small table; (2) gap year and rest: two friends sitting on a park bench beside walking backpacks and trees; (3) moving to rented housing: young adult with a housemate unpacking moving boxes in a small bright student room with bed and desk; (4) future savings goal: young adult at a table with a coin jar, small house model and a simple calendar with no writing.
Constraints: age-appropriate, ordinary Norwegian everyday life, diverse young adults, no dangerous or adult content, every scene visually distinct, consistent pixel size and camera. This is one atlas asset with sixteen equal cells, not a full-page illustration.
