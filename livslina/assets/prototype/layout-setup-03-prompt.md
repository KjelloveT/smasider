# Bildeprompt — prøve 03

Biletet vart laga med den innebygde `image_gen`-funksjonen. Først vart romkomposisjonen re-teikna i avgrensa 8-bit-stil. Deretter vart sofaen retta slik at sitjesida vender mot TV-en. Sluttbiletet `room/layout-setup-03-v2.png` vart redusert med næraste-nabo-metoden til 384 × 256 pikslar og kvantisert til høgst 128 fargar utan dithering, slik at det passar den logiske romflata.

## Komposisjons- og stilprompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: room-layout reference art for an 8-bit teen-room game. Input image: Image 1 is a composition reference only; preserve its corner-room viewing direction and the requested room arrangement, but re-render it in true crisp limited-palette pixel art. Primary request: make a clean new 3:2 landscape room scene with these four main objects clearly legible and correctly placed: one bed snug in the back-right corner, one writing desk on the right side in front of the bed, one compact two-seat sofa in the centre facing a small TV, and the TV on a low stand at the far left of a wide back-left-wall window. Keep the bed, sofa, desk and TV grounded on the same continuous floor plane and drawn from the same fixed slightly elevated corner camera, with consistent eye-height, perspective axes, scale, floor-contact shadows and upper-left light. The back corner is visible; wall verticals stay vertical; furniture must not look top-down or float. Style: authentic detailed 8-bit pixel art, crisp square pixel clusters on a visible 4-pixel logical grid, restrained palette, hard pixel edges with absolutely no antialiasing, painterly texture, soft gradients, blur, glossy highlights or subpixel noise. Use the reference palette family: deep plum-charcoal outlines, warm cream, coral, honey, sage green, muted blue and lavender; three clear shade steps per material. Composition: show full furniture and a broad clear walkway; simple background with plain sage walls and timber floor. Keep only the bed, desk, TV on a low stand, sofa, one window and restrained wall details; remove coffee table, extra chairs, shelves, excess plants, ceiling light and decorative clutter. The sofa must face toward the TV. The bed headboard is against the right wall in the far corner; its foot points toward the centre/front. The desk sits to the right of the sofa and forward of the bed. The TV is fully left of the window with a visible gap between them. Constraints: no people, text, labels, UI, brands, watermarks, extra furniture, duplicate objects, mirrored objects, cropped objects, or changes to the requested layout.
```

Change only the sofa's orientation and, if needed, shift it a few pixels within the same central rug zone. The sofa's open seating side must clearly face diagonally up-left toward the TV at the far left of the window; the backrest must be on the side away from the TV. Keep it a small two-seat sofa, at the same floor depth and pixel scale, with its feet grounded on the rug. Preserve the one fixed elevated corner camera and the same floor-plane perspective; rotate the sofa in the room, not the camera. Keep every other part of the image unchanged: room corner, window, TV and console, bed, desk and chair, walls, floor, rug, lighting, palette, pixel clusters, and object positions. Preserve the crisp low-resolution 8-bit pixel-art look with hard square pixel edges. Do not add objects, text, labels, blur, antialiasing, or a new viewpoint.
