# Ljodstigen – genererte bilete

Laga 14. oktober 2026 med det innebygde `image_gen`-verktøyet, éi køyring per bilete. Ingen CLI eller nye avhengnader.

Figurane er PNG med alfa, maks 384 × 384 px og 128 fargar. Spelbileta er JPEG, 480 × 360 px, kvalitet 82. Alle femten publiserte bileta er under 46 kB kvar; samla er dei kring 500 kB. Originalane ligg lokalt under `_kjelder/ljodstigen-bilete/` og er ikkje med i Git.

## Endelege biletpromptar

### Mose

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one original raster character cutout for the Norwegian children's reading game Ljodstigen. Create MOSE: a tiny friendly moss forest creature, soft pear-shaped green body, warm cream face and tummy, two large dark kind eyes, very short legs, two little leaf ears, simple warm ochre scarf. Front view, entire body and feet visible, neutral relaxed standing pose, centered in a square image, generous transparent padding on every side. Hand-painted storybook/game illustration with broad painted facets and soft gouache texture, confident dark green outer contour, clear simple silhouette readable at 56 pixels. Forest green, moss, cream, honey. Cheerful and curious, appropriate for ages 5–8. Original character, no resemblance to Poio characters, not a human in costume, no letters or text, no scenery, no frame, no cast shadow outside character. Genuine alpha transparency. This is the family anchor for 10 character cutouts, consistent proportions and lighting.

Same large head/small body proportions as the reference, broad painted facets, subtle natural gouache texture, warm gentle lighting, clear simple silhouette and thick dark-green outer contour readable at 56 pixels. Expressive but simple. Original character design for ages 5–8. No Poio characters or copied recognizable designs. Genuine alpha transparency. No scene, no typography, no letters, no border, no external shadow.
